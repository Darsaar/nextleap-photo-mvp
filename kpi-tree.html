One North Star, three steps that multiply into it, the inputs and events behind each step, and the guardrails that stop us chasing the wrong number. All targets are proposals, to be fixed after a two-week baseline.

North Star

Find rate

Photo-hunting sessions where the wanted photo is opened or shared within 60 seconds, divided by all photo-hunting sessions.

Find rate breaks into three steps that multiply

Describe rate × In top six × Recognised = Find rate

A lift early in the chain grows the pool every later step works on. Example: 1,000 hunts × 60% describe 2+ details × 70% shown in top six × 85% recognised ≈ 357 found.
